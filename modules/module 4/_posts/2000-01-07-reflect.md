### Module 4: Effectively Communicating Evidence of Practice
#### Reflect
*Suggested time: 10 minutes (individually) or more if discussing with a group*

Use these questions to self-reflect or share your thoughts with others.

* How did the communication process with your audience(s) go?
* What is **ONE** thing that went well?
* What is **ONE** thing that you would change in your communication if you had the chance to redo it?
* Did your communication accomplish the goal(s) you wanted? Why or why not?
* How could you share what you did and learned through this curriculum with the greater school library community (state or national organizations)?

> “While sharing evidence within one’s immediate school community is essential for the survival of the school library program, **sharing must occur across the profession in multiple forms**. Sharing is vital for the school library profession, and serves as a means for librarians to model librarian standards competencies within the “Collaborate” and “Engage” shared foundations and within the “Share” domain (AASL 2017).” (Moore et al., 2023, p. 71-72).
