### Module 4: Effectively Communicating Evidence of Practice
#### Module 4 Goals & Objectives
* 4.1: Describe the audience(s) you want (and need) to share your evidence of practice with
  * Identify at least one audience/stakeholder group you will share your evidence of practice with
  * Create a persona of this audience/stakeholder based on conversations, research, and your own experience
* 4.2: Translate data/evidence into meaningful information / Meaningfully share the evidence you’ve collected and analyzed with multiple audiences
  * Use the data you collect, organize, and analyze to tell a story that illustrates the work, value, and integration of the school library program with the school/district’s goals and strategic plans 
* 4.3: Create a product to communicate your evidence of practice
  * Explain and explore various media used to share evidence of practice
  * Assess which media would be best to use with your specific audience and situation
  * Use appropriate media to communicate your data story with your chosen audience
* 4.4: Communicate confidently with your audience/stakeholders
  * Incorporate emotional intelligence into communications with audience 

#### AASL School Librarian Standards* Addressed
* III.B.1. School librarians demonstrate the importance of personal, social, and intellectual networks by modeling the use of a variety of communication tools and resources.
* III.C.1. School librarians promote working productively with others to solve problems by demonstrating how to solicit and respond to feedback from others.
* VI.A.2. School librarians promote ethical and legal guidelines for gathering and using information by modeling the understanding of ethical use of information, technology, and media.
* VI.B.3. School librarians act as a resource for using valid information and reasoned conclusions to make ethical decisions in the creation of knowledge by promoting the inclusion of elements in personal-knowledge products that allow others to credit content appropriately.
* VI.C.2. School librarians promote the responsible, ethical, and legal sharing of new information with a global community by guiding the dissemination of new knowledge through means appropriate for the intended audience.

#### AASL School Library Standards* Addressed
* II.D.3. The school library builds empathy and equity within the global learning community by clearly and frequently articulating the school library’s impact when communicating with administration, faculty, staff, learners, parents, and the community.
* III.C.2. The school library provides opportunities for school librarians to connect and work with the learning community by designing and leading professional development opportunities that reinforce the impact of the school library’s resources, services, and programming on learners’ academic learning and educators’ effectiveness.
* V.C.3. The school library prepares learners to engage with a larger learning community by building and advocating for strong relationships with stakeholders who recognize and support an effective school library.
* VI.C.1. The school library encourages participation in an inclusive learning community to create and share information by providing both online and physical spaces for the sharing and dissemination of ideas and information

*based on the 2025 AASL National School Library Standards
