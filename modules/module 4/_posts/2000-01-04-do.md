### Module 4: Effectively Communicating Evidence of Practice
#### Do
Your “DO” task for this module is two-fold. First, you will **create a communication product** that clearly conveys the message you want to share with your chosen audience incorporating the evidence you have collected and analyzed in previous modules and using a medium that you think will best grab your audience’s attention. You can use one of the media above (or another of your choice). Then, you will **share your communication product with your audience.**

Let’s get started!

1. Fill out the [communications plan template](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qBeGyjERCAcl-FFqVdhuja5A6iOurKiZ/view?usp=sharing) (PDF) to help you create your communication product.
