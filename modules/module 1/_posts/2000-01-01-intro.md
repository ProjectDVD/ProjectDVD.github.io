### Module 1: Preparing for Evidence-Based Practice
#### Introduction
Before you dive head first into EBP, it is imperative to make a plan for why and how you are embarking on this EBP journey. When you travel, you most likely book a flight, reserve a hotel room, research local restaurants and different activities in the area. Every trip requires some pre-planning to ensure that you make the most of your precious time and money. Planning for EBP is no different. Planning ahead for EBP will assure that your EBP journey will go smoothly. In this module, you will identify your purpose for doing EBP and create an EBP plan to serve as a road map to guide you through the EBP process, so you can remain true to the overall goals you have for the school library, stay organized, and avoid overwhelm, stress, or abandoning EBP altogether.
