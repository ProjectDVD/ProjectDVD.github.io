### Reflect
*Suggested time: 10 minutes*

Take some time to think about your answers to the following questions:
1. Why is evidence of practice important in our profession and to your own practice as a school librarian?

Good questions from [Valenza (2015)](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1049167.pdf) to reflect on when planning for EBP

2. “How does my work [as a librarian] make a difference in improving teaching and learning?
3. What is my value [as a librarian] to [my school’s] learning culture?
4. How might I use evidence to improve my practice and enhance learning?”
