### Module 3: Analyzing Data & Interpreting Findings
#### Introduction
"Many librarians feel intimidated by data and statistics. It is important that we, as information professionals, gain a basic understanding, get past that fear, and understand what those basic concepts of statistics and data are." Jill Blaemers, from Data Quality Literacy: A Guidebook. 

You’ve collected your data. Now what? The next step is to analyze the data using the appropriate data analysis method and possibly special tools to assist with analysis. When you analyze data, you look for patterns and trends that may give you insight into what the data might mean. Once you have analyzed the data, you will interpret the findings (i.e. explain the patterns and trends you found in your analysis). You will ask yourself questions such as: What did my findings tell me? Were my goals met? Is additional work (e.g. further data collection or analysis), if any, needed?
