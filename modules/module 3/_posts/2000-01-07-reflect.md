### Module 3: Analyzing Data & Interpreting Findings
#### Reflect
*Suggested time: 10 minutes (individually) or more if discussing with a group*

Use these questions to self-reflect or share your thoughts with others.

* Which data analysis method do you feel most comfortable using? Why?
* Which data analysis method do you feel least comfortable using? Why?
* What would help you feel more confident in using that data analysis method?
* Was the data analysis method(s) you selected most appropriate for your data collection method? Why or why not?
* What story do your findings tell?
