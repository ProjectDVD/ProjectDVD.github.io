### Module 3: Analyzing Data & Interpreting Findings
#### Goals & Objectives
* 3.1: Define quantitative analysis.
* 3.2: Define qualitative analysis.
* 3.3: Identify the appropriate data analysis methods for the data you collected in Module #.
* 3.4: Select tools to aid with data analysis.
* 3.5: Analyze the data you collected in Module #. 
* 3.6: Provide context for findings. 
* 3.7: Explain the difference between correlation and causation.



#### AASL School Librarian Standards* Addressed
* III.C.1. School librarians promote working productively with others to solve problems by demonstrating how to solicit and respond to feedback from others.
* IV.A.1. School librarians challenge learners to act on an information need by modeling a response to a need to gather and organize information.
* VI.B.3. School librarians act as a resource for using valid information and reasoned conclusions to make ethical decisions in the creation of knowledge by promoting the inclusion of elements in personal-knowledge products that allow others to credit content appropriately
