### Module 2: Collecting Evidence of Practice
#### Reflect
*Suggested time: 10 minutes (individually) or more if discussing with a group*

Use these questions to self-reflect or share your thoughts with others.

- Was there anything that surprised you about the method you used to collect data?
- What was easy and/or challenging in collecting and organizing data?
- Does the data you collected show the impact that the library has on student learning? Are there other data that would better showcase the library’s impact on teaching and learning?
- Does the data you collected reflect all the students you want it to?*
- Are there ways you can streamline your data collection method(s)?*

*Adapted from Stoddart, R. (2023). We are the evidence: Uncovering everyday library practices through critical reflection. In C. Walker Wiley, A. B. Click, & M. Houlihan (Eds.), _Everyday evidence-based practice in academic libraries: Case studies and reflections_ (pp. 349-350). Association of College & Research Libraries.
