### Module 2: Collecting Evidence of Practice
#### Reflect
*Suggested time: 10 minutes*

Take some time to think about your answers to the following questions:

- Does the data you collected show the impact that the library has on student learning? Are there other data that would better showcase the library’s impact on teaching and learning?
- Does the data you collect reflect all the students you want it to?
- Are there ways you can streamline your data collection method(s)?
