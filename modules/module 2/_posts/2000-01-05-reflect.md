### Module 2: Collecting Evidence of Practice
#### Reflect
*Suggested time: 10 minutes*

Take some time to think about your answers to the following questions:

1. Does the data you collected show the impact that the library has on student learning? Are there other data that would better showcase the library’s impact on teaching and learning?
1. Does the data you collect reflect all the students you want it to?
1. Are there ways you can streamline your data collection method(s)?
