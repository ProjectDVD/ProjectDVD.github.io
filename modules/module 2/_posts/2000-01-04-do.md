> **Where can I get help with collecting data?**
> - **Your principal:** Your principal likely thinks about student data A LOT. He/she probably knows the subjects and topics in which students struggle and the ones in which they excel. Your principal would have a macro view of each grade level’s strengths and areas for improvement.
> - **Teachers:** If you want to know if students are integrating what they learn about the inquiry process or various other information literacy skills into their work, collaborating with teachers on including these skills into the unit and assessment rubric is an excellent way to do this.
